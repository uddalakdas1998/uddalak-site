Index

A — The person behind the output

Research, building,
pictures, music.
All of it, at once.

I'm Uddalak. I keep four things running in parallel and I've stopped trying to rank them — each one makes the others sharper. Cancer biology and computational drug discovery is the discipline I trained in and still practise daily.

The rest isn't a hobby shelf. I build ventures because a good idea deserves to exist somewhere other than a paper. I shoot as @pixapot because attention is a single muscle and it needs subjects that won't hold still. I sing because it's the oldest habit I have. I travel because the next city I haven't worked in is the one dataset I don't have yet. The framing I learned behind a camera is the framing I use on a figure.

Off the bench

MUSIC

I sing, and I listen hard

Singing is the oldest habit I have. Music is how the reading gets done and how the writing gets finished — and a good live set beats most conferences.

TRAVEL

Travel is the other half of the camera. Every new city is a new dataset, and the best ideas have all arrived somewhere other than a desk.

              V — What I've started

              Ideas are cheap.

              isn't.
